Who we are

You don't have a data problem. You have a coherence problem.

A tool here, an analyst there, one heroic spreadsheet holding it all together — that doesn't add up to a strategy. Wilbur Dekum helps small and midsize organizations and non-profits replace piecemeal efforts with a coherent data foundation you can actually build on. Because using data well is no longer an advantage. It's a requirement.

What we do

Data Strategy & Assessment

How we think

A point of view, not a playbook.

Good data work is less about technology and more about coherence. Every piece connected, from how data is created to how a decision gets made.

I.Coherence over piecemeal

An analyst without infrastructure. A warehouse without governance. Partial fixes fail, so we treat data as one connected system, end to end.

II.A requirement, not a luxury

Every organization is now expected to run on data. We're the catch-up path for teams who can't pause the business to build it.

III.The plan is yours

Assessment first, always. You get the map whether or not you hire us to walk it. And we'll say so when the right answer is hiring someone instead.

IV.Every altitude

Strategic and tactical, weeds to clouds. Comfortable with your board deck and your dbt repo.

How we work

Engagement model

A clear path from first conversation to lasting capability, with no black boxes along the way.

01

Assess

Two to six weeks of interviews across every relevant function. Each conversation shapes the next, until we hold a picture of your data landscape no single person had.

02

Plan

A live readout and a detailed, staged operational plan covering infrastructure, tooling, staffing, metrics, and process. Practical and specific.

03

Build

The plan is yours. Implement it in-house, use it as a hiring roadmap, or engage us for the subset that fits your budget and timeline.

04

Lead

Fractional data leadership on a monthly, days-per-week basis. We work with your team and bring in trusted engineers and data scientists when the work calls for it.
